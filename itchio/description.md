**RPG Adventure** is a turn-based fantasy RPG you can play solo or with friends, across two very
different worlds: a Middle-earth-inspired realm of orcs, wraiths, and healers, or a Pokémon-style
world of trainers and creatures.

Gather a party, explore a pixel-art overworld, and fight your way through scenario-based campaigns
— dodge, block, land criticals, use potions, and level up as you go. Equip gear, spend talent
points, and restock at the shop between fights.

**Features**
- Turn-based tactical combat — attacks, dodges, blocks, critical hits, and consumables all matter
- Two selectable universes: Middle-earth-style fantasy, and a Pokémon-style creature-collector setting
- Top-down overworld exploration with random encounters, NPCs, and locked areas
- Multiplayer (each player controls their own hero) or single-player (control the whole party yourself)
- Talent trees, equipment, a shop, and a full inventory system
- Music and sound effects for combat, healing, potions, victory, and defeat
- English and French
- Windows, Linux, and Android

**This is an online game** — it connects to our server to play, so you'll need an internet connection.
