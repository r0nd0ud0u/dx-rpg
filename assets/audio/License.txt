	The Kenney/OpenGameArt files above are placeholder picks — swap any file
	under assets/audio/ for a better-fitting one at any time; the code only
	depends on the filenames listed above, not their content.

	The synthesised sfx listed above are our own, and are released under the
	same CC0 terms. They are generated rather than sampled: re-run
	`python3 scripts/gen_sfx.py` to rebuild them all, or name one
	(`... gen_sfx.py strike`) to rebuild just that one, after editing its
	synthesis recipe in that script. Which sound a given attack plays is
	decided in src/sfx_cue.rs.
